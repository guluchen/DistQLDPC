Original local error-frame MaxSAT encoding, adjoint ideal-output sweep, and
bounded demand-driven connected-cluster responses. Production circuit paths
do not enumerate the complete detector-error matrix. No external solver is
linked or used by this web application.

Detector/logical distance formulation: M. A. Webster et al., Distance-Finding
Algorithms for Quantum Codes and Circuits, arXiv:2603.22532.
Circuit-distance and benchmark reference: Bin Cheng, Naixu Guo and Feng Pan,
tropidist, arXiv:2611.08247 (source review b03ec2d). No tropidist code copied.
Stim format/gate interoperability follows quantumlib/Stim documentation;
no simulator implementation was copied or linked.
