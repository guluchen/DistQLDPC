MODEL CONTRACT
The distance belongs to the experiment.
What is being minimized?
The number of allowed fault locations that flip at least one declared logical observable while every detector remains quiet. Pauli alternatives at the same location are mutually exclusive. Nonzero probabilities define support; they do not change unit costs.
What defines a circuit?
Supply the preparation and measurement boundaries, detector and observable annotations, and explicit noise locations. The generated memory circuit uses a clean preparation round, serial X-then-Z checks, and perfect final data readout. It is a reference schedule, not a fault-tolerance guarantee.
What is supported?
I, X, Y, Z, H, S, S_DAG, CX, CZ, SWAP, R/RX, M/MX, MR/MRX; Pauli/depolarizing noise, bounded REPEAT, and record annotations. Feedback, leakage and non-Clifford instructions are rejected. Browser limits: 100,000 operations / fault alternatives and 2 million signature entries.
Supported formats & result guarantees
Native JSON uses distqldpc-circuit-v1 or distqldpc-fault-v1. Sparse faults have group, detectors and observables; group -1 means independent. Every selected alternative costs one.
Ideal detector and observable parities must be deterministic. Every optimal witness is checked for detector parity, logical parity, cost and exclusivity; circuit witnesses are also replayed forward. The optimality claim relies on complete MaxSAT search. There is no proof-file certificate in this release.
Time limit or Stop yields no exact distance. “No allowed fault set” is infeasibility under the specified model, not distance zero. This tool computes undetectable-logical fault distance, not a decoder’s failure distance. It still stores fault signatures and expands bounded repeats; no speed advantage over Stim is claimed.
